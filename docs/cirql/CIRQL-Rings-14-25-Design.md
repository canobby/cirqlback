# CIRQLVERSE — Rings 14–25 Design (12 all-new exotic shores)

Twelve brand-new, hand-authored rings beyond the biome ring (0–13). These are **destinations, not templates**: each is an exotic biome we haven't built, with its own **landmark set-piece**, a **marquee mystery/choice quest** (the headline), quick **palate-cleanser** shorts, a **difficult multi-stage co-op campaign**, a **sub-realm payoff**, and a small **named cast**. Together with the 12 biome rings, this gives **24 distinct rings** to work with.

> **Direction locked with the owner:** all-new exotic biomes · quest variety leans **Mystery + Choice** · CIRQLSPHERE is the umbrella brand over all games (wordmark pass deferred until after the game work).

## Design rules for every ring here

- **A marquee quest that's a real story** — a multi-beat mystery with a **branching choice** at the end that shifts reward *and* standing (like *The Sunken Runes*, but per ring).
- **Short↔long rhythm** — each big arc is surrounded by 1–2 quick quests so it never grinds.
- **Outer-ring co-op campaigns** — difficult, Renown-gated party expeditions (we currently have *zero* off the Town).
- **Sub-realm payoffs** — every cave/hollow-tree/cloud-stair earns its keep (a vault, a boss, or a postcard vista).
- **Biome-aware naming + a named cast** so each shore is a place.
- **Build-reward ties** — several marquees drop biome-flavoured décor/materials for your CIRQLSPACE.
- **Choice never punishes hard** — the fork trades *flavour* (a cosmetic / an ally / a lore beat) against *Renown*, so both paths feel good.

## The new quest shapes (mystery + choice first)

| Shape | Feel | Used as |
|---|---|---|
| **Mystery** | Follow a multi-clue trail, talk to a cast, deduce | Most marquees |
| **Choice** | A fork that shifts reward + standing | Ending beat of most marquees |
| Restore/Build | Repair a landmark in stages → drops CIRQLSPACE décor | Some campaigns |
| Escort | Lead a creature/NPC past hazards | Some shorts |
| Timed | Beat a tide / bolt / geyser | Sprinkled shorts + a few campaigns |
| Boss/Trial | A sub-realm challenge | The hardest campaigns |

# Ring 14 — Glassmere  🔷  *singing glass-dune desert*

**Look:** dunes of wind-fused glass that chime in the wind; prismatic pale-cyan/rose refractions; glass-reeds and crystal-cacti; wind-carved glass arches; heat-mirage shimmer (no water). **Hero fauna:** a glass-scaled sand-skink that rings faintly as it moves.

**Landmark — The Prism Monolith:** a giant standing glass shard at the hub that splits daylight into a rainbow fan across the dunes.

**Marquee — "The Colour That Isn't" (mystery → choice):** the Monolith casts six colours, but at dusk a hidden **seventh beam** appears. Track where it lands across three dunes to uncover a buried chime-shrine. **Choice:** *ring the shrine* (wakes the desert's song — a music/cosmetic reward) or *let it sleep* (Renown + a lore fragment about who tuned the dunes).

**Shorts:** *Tune the Reeds* (walk past 4 glass-reeds in pitch order) · *Chase the Mirage* (a quick reach).

**Campaign — "Refraction" (Epic · Rank 3):** a party aligns 3 movable prisms to bend the Monolith's beam onto a sealed vault — a light-puzzle solved together.

**Sub-realm (cave) — the Geode Hollow:** walls of singing crystal; a 360° postcard chamber.

**Cast:** Sable the Glasswright (keeper) · Echo, a child who can *hear* the seventh colour.

# Ring 15 — Sporehaven  🍄  *towering bioluminescent fungal jungle*

**Look:** giant glowing mushroom-**towers** you walk beneath (vertical, unlike the flat marsh); constant drifting spore-rain motes; teal/violet canopy light. **Hero fauna:** a spore-moth swarm and a slow, luminous glow-snail.

**Landmark — The Motherbloom:** a colossal pulsing fungal spire at the hub, breathing light.

**Marquee — "The Rot & the Bloom" (mystery → choice):** the Motherbloom is dimming. Investigate three lesser blooms; one is infected. **Choice:** *burn the infected bloom* (fast cure, but a grove is lost) or *nurse it* (a longer multi-visit cure that saves the grove — more Renown).

**Shorts:** *Follow the Spores* (a gather that drifts on the wind) · *Cap the Sprouts* (quick interact).

**Campaign — "Deep Bloom" (Epic · Rank 4):** descend the hollow-tree sub-realm to relight the mycelium network node-by-node, past a fungal-guardian gate.

**Sub-realm (hollow tree) — the Mycelium Deep:** an underground-glow chamber threaded with light-veins.

**Cast:** Fenwick the Mycologist · Mold, a grumpy spore-tender.

# Ring 16 — Mirrormere Flats  🪞  *white salt mirror-lake*

**Look:** an endless white salt flat under a mirror-thin water sheen that doubles the whole sky; shimmering mirages; bone-white + sky-mirror palette. **Hero fauna:** a long-legged mirror-heron that appears to walk on its own reflection.

**Landmark — The Doubled Gate:** an arch whose reflection is a *different* arch.

**Marquee — "Which Sky Is Real" (the signature choice quest):** a traveller's reflection has gone missing. Follow the places where reflection and reality quietly disagree to unmask a **mirage-thief**. **Choice:** *give them your reflection* (they become real — you lose a cosmetic slot for a day) or *bind them* (they stay a ghost — Renown + their story).

**Shorts:** *Step Only on Solid Sky* (a light reach-puzzle across mirror tiles).

**Campaign — "The Long Mirror" (Epic · Rank 3):** the party crosses the flats by staying inside each other's reflections — co-op positioning.

**Sub-realm (cloud stair) — the Sky-Overlook:** where the mirror-sea meets the real one (postcard).

**Cast:** Vesper, a keeper with no reflection · the Mirage.

# Ring 17 — Stormstep  ⚡  *perpetual-lightning steppe*

**Look:** a dark grass steppe under constant sheet-lightning; **fulgurite** glass spires where bolts struck; static motes; bruise-violet + electric-white palette. **Hero fauna:** a static-charged jackrabbit that sparks when it hops.

**Landmark — The Iron Tree:** a lightning-scarred metal-bark tree that rings like a bell when struck.

**Marquee — "Where Lightning Remembers" (mystery → choice):** the strikes aren't random — they spell a path. Map three strike-sites to find a buried **sky-anchor**. **Choice:** *raise the anchor* (calms the storm, opens a hidden grove) or *leave it* (an endless-storm cosmetic + Renown).

**Shorts:** *Beat the Bolt* (a timed dash between strikes).

**Campaign — "Grounded" (Epic · Rank 4):** a party plants 3 lightning rods to safely reach the Iron Tree — a defend-style coordination run.

**Sub-realm (cave) — the Fulgurite Cavern:** a lattice of glass tubes fused by ancient bolts.

**Cast:** Bram the Stormwarden · Flint, a fearless rod-runner.

# Ring 18 — The Leviathan Coast  🦴  *giant fossil-bone shore*

**Look:** a beach of colossal fossil ribs arching over the sand, ancient leviathan skulls, tidepools inside the eye-sockets; bleached-bone + deep-sea-teal glow; a distant whale-song ambience. **Hero fauna:** scuttling bone-crabs.

**Landmark — The Ribcage Cathedral:** you walk *inside* a leviathan's ribs at the hub.

**Marquee — "The Last Song of the Deep" (mystery → choice):** reassemble a leviathan's death-song from four bone-flutes scattered along the shore, then play it at the cathedral. **Choice:** *lay the spirit to rest* (Renown + a calm falls over the coast) or *record its song* (a new music theme / cosmetic — ties into the soundtrack).

**Shorts:** *Tidepool Census* (a quick gather in the eye-socket pools).

**Campaign — "Leviathan Rising" (Epic · Rank 5 · boss):** the party wakes a guardian bone-serpent in the cave sub-realm and must out-position it.

**Sub-realm (cave) — the Marrow-Deep:** glowing with abyssal light.

**Cast:** Ossa the Bonesinger · a hermit called Barnacle.

# Ring 19 — The Floating Isles (Skardrift)  ☁  *chunks of land afloat over a cloud-sea*

**Look:** broken islets hovering over a cloud-sea, linked by **updraft columns** you ride between them; petals and leaves drift *upward*; dawn-gold + sky-blue. **Hero fauna:** a gliding sky-manta drifting between isles.

**Landmark — The Anchor Stone:** the one isle still chained to the world — a windmill-shrine.

**Marquee — "What Holds the Sky" (mystery → choice):** the isles are drifting apart. Read the wind-patterns to find which of three anchor-chains is fraying. **Choice:** *re-anchor it* (stabilises the ring, opens a far isle) or *cut it free* (a drifting-isle sanctuary + Renown).

**Shorts:** *Catch the Updraft* (a short vertical traversal).

**Campaign — "Isle-Hop" (Epic · Rank 4 · timed):** a party relay across five isles via timed updrafts.

**Sub-realm (cloud stair) — the Thunderhead Vista:** above the isles, the world spread below (postcard).

**Cast:** Zephyr the Windwright · Wisp, a kite-maker.

# Ring 20 — Amberhold  🟠  *resin forest where life is frozen in glowing amber*

**Look:** a forest where creatures and old light are trapped in **glowing amber resin**; warm internal gold light; slow amber-drips; honey-gold + deep-brown palette. **Hero fauna:** a recently-freed amber-moth, still shedding resin-dust.

**Landmark — The Heartwood Amber:** a house-sized amber block with *something moving inside*.

**Marquee — "What Sleeps in Amber" (mystery → choice, big):** four amber pods hold four sleepers; the tablet warns only one should wake. Read each sleeper's dream (branching dialog) to judge who's benign, then **choose who to wake at the Heartwood** — one becomes a rare ally NPC, one is a trick that costs sparqs; the clues tell them apart.

**Shorts:** *Free the Light* (tap 3 small amber-glimmers loose).

**Campaign — "The Long Thaw" (Epic · Rank 5 · restore):** melt the resin channels with lantern-heat in the right sequence to free a trapped grove.

**Sub-realm (hollow tree) — the Amber Vault:** a chamber of frozen fireflies.

**Cast:** Rue the Amberkeep · a sleeper known only as The Guest.

# Ring 21 — The Verdigris  🏛  *a fallen CIRQL ring-city reclaimed by luminous moss*

**Look:** an ancient collapsed ring-city overgrown with glowing verdigris moss and vines; broken columns, a sunken plaza, a dead fountain; copper-green + rose-bloom palette. **Hero fauna:** moss-deer picking through the ruins (a woodland callback, recoloured).

**Landmark — The Broken Rotunda:** a half-domed civic hall with a silent fountain.

**Marquee — "Who Lived Here" (mystery, lore-heavy → choice):** restore three mural fragments and read the city's fall — the truth reframes the world's history. **Choice:** *restore the fountain* (the city partly reawakens — seeds future sub-shops) or *leave it a memorial* (Renown + a solemn cosmetic).

**Shorts:** *Rubbing of the Glyph* (a quick interact set across broken tablets).

**Campaign — "Reclamation" (Epic · Rank 6 · restore):** a party clears four rubble-gates to reopen the old ring-road.

**Sub-realm (cave) — the Undercroft Archive:** glowing shelves of lost knowledge.

**Cast:** Cartogra, an old keeper who *remembers* · Coin, a scavenger.

# Ring 22 — Tanglemere Mangrove  🌿  *a half-submerged glowing mangrove maze*

**Look:** a maze of luminous mangrove roots over black water, broken boardwalk fragments, drifting lanterns; teal-black water + firefly-gold light. **Hero fauna:** a glow-otter that will lead you through the maze.

**Landmark — The Lantern Tree:** a mangrove hung with a hundred drifting lights at the hub.

**Marquee — "The False Light" (mystery → choice):** someone is moving the guide-lanterns to lead travellers astray. Track the pattern and catch the culprit — a lonely marsh-sprite. **Choice:** *report them* (Renown) or *teach them to guide instead* (they become a helpful sub-realm guide NPC).

**Shorts:** *Follow the Otter* (an escort short — keep up as it leads you out).

**Campaign — "The Deepwater Run" (Epic · Rank 4 · timed):** the party threads the root-maze together, each carrying a light, before the tide rises.

**Sub-realm (hollow tree) — the Root-Cathedral:** a vaulted chamber beneath the water.

**Cast:** Marsh the Lamplighter · Ember-of-the-Fen, the sprite.

# Ring 23 — Frostfire Rift  🌋❄  *a rift where glacier meets lava*

**Look:** a jagged rift where glaciers pour into lava channels; steam geysers, obsidian-and-ice spires, purple-white steam glow; a palette split down the middle — ice-blue / ember-orange. **Hero fauna:** a "cinder-fox" (a fox/salamander hybrid recolour) at home in the steam.

**Landmark — The Seam:** where a wall of ice meets a wall of fire and neither wins.

**Marquee — "The Balance Keeps" (mystery → choice):** the Seam is tipping toward fire. Investigate three vents to find which broke. **Choice:** *cool it* (ice wins — a frozen grove + ice-décor drop) or *let it burn* (a lava grove + ember-décor drop) — each opens a different reward path.

**Shorts:** *Mind the Geyser* (a timed hop across erupting vents).

**Campaign — "Walk the Seam" (Epic · Rank 6 · trial):** the party crosses the rift alternating ice/fire safe-tiles in sync — a hard co-op positioning trial.

**Sub-realm (cave) — the Steam Grotto:** mineral colour blooming in the mist.

**Cast:** Solstice, who tends the balance · the twins Ash & Frost.

# Ring 24 — The Gilded Waste (Sunken Palace)  👑  *gold dunes over a half-buried palace*

**Look:** rolling gold-dust dunes half-swallowing a vast buried palace; gilded spires poking through the sand; sunset palette + gold glow. **Hero fauna:** a jewelled desert-fox (a gilded fennec recolour).

**Landmark — The Throne in the Sand:** a half-buried golden throne room.

**Marquee — "The King Under the Dune" (mystery → choice, major):** uncover the fate of the buried court through four relics, each a small mystery. At the throne, **choose:** *claim the crown* (a power/cosmetic reward — but the sand keeps rising) or *seal the palace* (Renown + a guardian's blessing).

**Shorts:** *Dig for Glimmers* (a gather in the shifting dunes).

**Campaign — "The Excavation" (Epic · Rank 7 · restore + boss):** a multi-stage party dig — clear the sand from three gates, then descend to the vault guardian.

**Sub-realm (cave) — the Treasure Vault:** a heist-puzzle payoff behind the throne.

**Cast:** Nyx the Duneseer · Marigold, a would-be heir.

# Ring 25 — Nightglass Caverns (The Hollow Reach)  🌑  *a walkable blacklight-cavern ring — the finale*

**Look:** perpetual twilight under a cavern-dark sky, everything lit by **crystal veins and glow-fungus** — the true blacklight-cave feel that started the whole glow-up, but as an open, walkable shore; near-black palette shot through with neon crystal. **Hero fauna:** a blind crystal-moth that navigates by *your* light.

**Landmark — The Heartcrystal:** a cathedral-sized crystal at the hub that pulses with the world's own light.

**Marquee — "The Last Light" (mystery → choice, finale):** the Heartcrystal is fading. Gather three lost light-shards, each guarded by a small mystery elsewhere on the ring, and rekindle it. **Final choice (an ending beat):** *share the light outward* (the whole ring blooms into a spectacular payoff) or *keep it contained* (a private sanctuary + top-tier Renown).

**Shorts:** *Light the Way* (kindle scattered glow-fungus clusters).

**Campaign — "Into the Reach" (Epic · Rank 7 · the toughest):** a full-party expedition through the caverns to the Heartcrystal, with a light-management mechanic and a final guardian.

**Sub-realm (cave) — the Deep Vein:** the brightest, most beautiful chamber in the world — the ultimate postcard.

**Cast:** Lumen the Lightkeeper, the world's oldest keeper · Spark, an echo.

# Rollout notes (for when we build)

- **These layer on the generator** — each ring still gets its procedural side quests for coverage; the marquee + campaign + landmark are the authored layer on top.
- **Order of build:** biome art kit → landmark set-piece → marquee quest (new mystery/choice quest engine) → campaign → sub-realm payoff → cast/dialog.
- **New engine work needed first:** a **branching mystery/choice quest type** (clue objectives + a fork that writes a flag + grants one of two rewards) — reusable across all 12. This is the one real system to add before authoring.
- **Naming fix:** adopt biome-aware naming for these (and retro-fit rings 2–13) so names match their shore.
