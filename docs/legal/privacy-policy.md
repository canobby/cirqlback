**[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]** ("**Cirqlback**," "**we**," "**us**") respects your privacy. This Policy explains what we collect, why, how we use and share it, and your choices. It applies to the Cirqlback websites, apps, and tap-to-earn Service. By using the Service you agree to this Policy.

We built Cirqlback to be **privacy-light by design**: customers can tap and earn without creating a traditional account, and we ask for the minimum information needed to run the Service.

## 1. Information we collect

**You give us:**
- **Account details** — for businesses and coordinators: name, email, business information, and login credentials. For customers: an **optional** email (many features work anonymously).
- **Content** — business listings, hosted-page content, images, badge art, messages, and feedback.
- **Payment information** — handled by **Stripe**. We do **not** store full card numbers; we receive limited billing metadata (e.g., last four digits, status) from Stripe.

**We collect automatically:**
- **Tap / activity data** — when you tap an NFC tag or scan a QR code: which tag/business, timestamp, points earned, streak and reward progress.
- **Approximate location** — for the discovery map and location-based features, where you permit it. You can decline location access in your device/browser.
- **Device and usage data** — IP address, device/browser type, pages viewed, and similar log data, used for security, analytics, and reliability.
- **Cookies and similar technologies** — see Section 6.

**From third parties:**
- Payment and payout status from Stripe; mapping data from our maps provider; limited data from a business or coordinator who lists or refers you.

We do **not** knowingly collect sensitive categories (e.g., precise health, biometric, or government-ID data) through normal use.

## 2. How we use information

- Operate the Service: award points/rewards/badges/streaks, run campaigns, power the discovery map, and process payments and coordinator payouts.
- Provide support and the optional in-app AI Help Assistant (see Section 5).
- Protect the Service: detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and reward manipulation, and enforce our Terms.
- Improve the Service: analytics and product development.
- Communicate: service messages, and — where permitted — updates and offers you can opt out of.
- Comply with law and enforce our rights.

We rely on the legal bases of **performance of a contract**, **legitimate interests** (security, improvement), **consent** (e.g., location, marketing), and **legal obligation**, as applicable.

## 3. How we share information

We do **not sell** your personal information for money. We share it only:

- **With businesses and coordinators** you interact with — a business can see activity relevant to its own loyalty program (e.g., taps and rewards at that business); a coordinator can see activity within their territory. We share the minimum needed and, for customers, favor aggregated or pseudonymous data.
- **With service providers** ("processors") who run parts of the Service under contract — e.g., **Stripe** (payments/payouts), our **hosting** provider, our **maps** provider, and our **AI** provider for the optional assistant. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
- **For legal reasons** — to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Service.
- **In a business transfer** — as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.
