Answers to common questions about Cirqlback. For step-by-step help, use the **"Need help?"** assistant inside your dashboard, or see the [user guides](../manuals/). For legal details, see our [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md) and [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

## For customers

**What is Cirqlback?**
A local loyalty and discovery app. You **tap** an NFC tag (or scan a QR code) at a participating shop to earn points, rewards, streaks, and badges — and to discover more local businesses.

**Do I need an app or an account?**
No app to install, and you can start anonymously. You can optionally add an email to save your progress across devices and unlock more features.

**How do I earn points?**
Tap at participating businesses. You can also earn from streaks, referrals, the daily spin, seasonal events, and completing passports/collections.

**What can I do with points?**
Redeem them for business perks and platform rewards, and enter prize draws. Points have **no cash value** and can't be exchanged for money.

**Do points or rewards expire?**
They can, depending on the reward and any promotion. Check the reward details. See the [Terms](terms-of-service.md) for the full rules.

**Is my data private?**
We're privacy-light by design — anonymous by default, minimal data, and we don't sell your personal information for money. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

**Is tapping safe?**
Yes. An NFC tap just opens a web link; it can't access your personal data or install anything.

## For businesses

**What does Cirqlback cost?**
There's a free **Starter** trial, then **Core** and **Pro** subscriptions, plus optional add-ons. See the price sheet or the [Merchant Agreement](merchant-agreement.md).

**What's the difference between Core and Pro?**
Pro includes more locations, a hosted business page (free on Pro), priority support, and the ability to create multi-store campaigns. Core covers the essentials. Your dashboard shows exactly what your plan includes.

**Do I need special hardware?**
Just NFC tags or printed QR codes at your counter — no POS integration required.

**How do multi-store campaigns share reward costs?**
When several stores fund a shared reward, the cost is split **tap-weighted** across the stores a customer visited, the host fronts it, and settlements are reconciled and paid via Stripe. See the [Merchant Agreement](merchant-agreement.md), Section 4.

**How do I get paid when I host a funded reward?**
Through **Stripe Connect** to your connected account, after completing Stripe's onboarding.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Subscriptions auto-renew until you cancel; cancellation takes effect at the end of your billing period. See the [Merchant Agreement](merchant-agreement.md), Section 2.

## For Community Coordinators

**What is a Coordinator?**
A local partner (an **independent contractor**) who grows and supports Cirqlback businesses in a territory and earns a **revenue share** (default **70%**).

**Am I an employee?**
No — you're a **1099 independent contractor**. You control how you work, use your own equipment, and handle your own taxes. See the [1099 Guide](coordinator-1099-guide.md) and [Contractor Agreement](coordinator-independent-contractor-agreement.md).

**How and when do I get paid?**
You earn a share of revenue actually collected from businesses in your territory, reconciled to a statement and paid out on Cirqlback's schedule. Refunds/chargebacks reduce the associated share.

**Will I get a tax form?**
If you're paid $600+ in a year, you'll receive a **1099-NEC**. Set aside part of each payout for taxes — see the [1099 Guide](coordinator-1099-guide.md).
