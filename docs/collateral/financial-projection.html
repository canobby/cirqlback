Illustrative unit economics & a scalable territory model · monthly figures unless noted · USD
Model assumptions. Core $19.99/mo, Pro $49.99/mo; customers free. Add‑on attach assumed $8/mo avg per Core and $10/mo avg per Pro (≈0.5–0.7 add‑ons). Territory business mix 70% Core / 30% Pro. Community Coordinators keep 70% of their territory's gross; the platform keeps 30% (and 100% of any direct, non‑territory business). Figures ignore Stripe fees (~2.9%+30¢) and the yearly discount (2 months free); most businesses pay monthly.
